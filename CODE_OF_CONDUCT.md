# Code of conduct

Treat other contributors with respect. Discuss the work and give specific, constructive
feedback. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination and sharing someone else's private
information without permission are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained for Sunny.
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when someone violates them.

Flag harmful content using [GitHub's report tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
When you are comfortable doing so, you can also flag the behavior for the maintainer in the
related thread. Keep personal information out of public reports.
